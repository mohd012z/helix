Data provenance rules

DAILY_CLOSE is not REAL_TIME

Scheduled historical prices cannot become live merely by refreshing the browser.

Imported ≠ verified

Local files may contain stale or incorrect data. The UI labels them unverified.

No trading endpoints

This workspace makes no orders and requests no wallet or brokerage credentials.
